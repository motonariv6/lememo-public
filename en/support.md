# Le’memo Support

Thank you for using Le’memo.

If you need help with the app, subscriptions, purchases, cloud sync, or other issues, please see the information below.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I purchase or restore a subscription?

You can purchase a plan from the subscription screen in the app.

If you previously purchased a subscription using the same Apple Account or Google account, please try the “Restore Purchases” option in the app.

You can also review your subscription status and billing information in the App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

### How do I cancel a subscription?

Please cancel your subscription from the App Store or Google Play subscription management screen.

Deleting or uninstalling the app does not automatically cancel your subscription.

### Cloud sync is not working

Please check your network connection, restart the app, and try again.

If the issue continues, please include the following information when contacting support:

- Le’memo app version
- iOS / Android version
- Device model
- Description of the issue
- Screenshot showing the issue, if available

### Where can I learn about data and privacy?

Please see the [Privacy Policy]({{ '/en/privacy/' | relative_url }}) for information about how Le’memo handles data.
