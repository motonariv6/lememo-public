# Le’memo Account Deletion

Le’memo allows users to permanently delete their account directly from the app.

Deleting your account removes your Le’memo account and associated cloud data.

## Delete Your Account in the App

1. Open Le’memo
2. Open Settings
3. Select Account
4. Tap Delete Account
5. Review the confirmation messages and confirm deletion

After successful deletion, your Le’memo account and associated data are removed and the app returns to an unsigned-in state.

## Request Account Deletion on the Web

If you cannot access the app, you may request account deletion by email:

[Request account deletion by email](mailto:motonariv6@gmail.com?subject=Le%27memo%20Account%20Deletion%20Request)

Please include the email address of the Google account used with Le’memo.

We may request additional information to verify account ownership.

## Data Deleted

Account deletion removes data associated with your Le’memo account, including:

- Le’memo user account
- Synced person and profile information
- Relationship categories
- Notes, impressions, and interaction records
- Outings, schedules, visits, and customer visit records
- Expense and sales records
- Cloud synchronization state
- Authentication and recovery-related account information
- Le’memo user data stored on the device
- Photos and media managed by Le’memo

## Data Not Deleted

Purchase and payment records maintained by Google Play or the Apple App Store are managed separately by those platforms.

Deleting your Le’memo account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play or App Store subscription.

Please manage or cancel subscriptions through the relevant store subscription settings.

## Processing Time

Deletion initiated in the app is generally processed immediately when successful.

Email deletion requests are processed within a reasonable period after account ownership has been verified.
