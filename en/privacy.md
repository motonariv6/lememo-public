# Le’memo Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: October 1, 2026**  
**Last Updated: October 1, 2026**

Yusuke Mori (the “Operator”) establishes this Privacy Policy regarding the handling of information in the application “Le’memo” (the “Service”).

## 1. Basic Policy

The Operator handles information processed through the Service appropriately according to its nature and in accordance with applicable laws and other relevant requirements.

## 2. Information Processed by the Service

### 2.1 Information Entered or Stored by Users

Depending on how the Service is used, the following information may be processed or stored locally on the User’s device:

- Display name and other profile information
- Names, display names, nicknames, or other identifiers of people recorded by the User
- Birthdays and anniversaries
- Relationship categories and notes
- Conversation content, events, impressions, and ratings
- Hobbies, preferences, dislikes, and other attributes
- Outings, trips, dates, places, and visit history
- Expenses, payments, gifts, and other monetary information
- Photos and other media
- Other information voluntarily entered, imported, or stored by the User

This information may include information about third parties other than the User.

### 2.2 Device, App, and Settings Information

The Service may process information such as:

- App settings
- Language settings
- Nightlife visibility settings and other display settings
- Subscription status
- App version
- Operating system, device type, and other technical information
- Technical information required to investigate errors or failures

### 2.3 Purchase and Subscription Information

The Service may process the following information through Apple, Google, and RevenueCat in connection with purchases and subscriptions:

- Purchased product
- Subscription entitlement and active status
- Renewal, cancellation, expiration, or similar status
- Transaction-related information such as purchase date
- Identifiers issued by RevenueCat
- Purchase-related information provided by the App Store or Google Play

Payment credentials such as credit-card numbers are generally processed directly by Apple, Google, or the applicable payment platform and are not directly collected by the Operator.

## 3. Purposes of Use

The Operator may use information processed through the Service for the following purposes:

1. To provide the Service
2. To save, display, organize, and search information entered by Users
3. To verify subscription entitlements
4. To provide purchase restoration and other billing-related functionality
5. To investigate defects, respond to incidents, and improve the Service
6. To respond to inquiries from Users
7. To prevent fraud, misuse, or security issues
8. To respond to conduct that violates applicable law or the Terms of Use

## 4. Local Storage

1. At present, the primary user data handled by the Service is generally stored locally on the User’s device.
2. The Operator does not ordinarily access the contents of data stored only on the User’s device in the course of providing the Service.
3. If a User voluntarily provides data to the Operator for support, backup, export, or other purposes, the Operator may process that data within the scope necessary for such purpose.

## 5. Processing of Purchase Information by RevenueCat

1. The Service uses RevenueCat to manage subscription status, purchases, restorations, and related billing functionality.
2. RevenueCat may process purchase status, products, renewal or cancellation status, expiration status, identifiers, and other purchase-related information.
3. RevenueCat’s handling of information is governed by RevenueCat’s own privacy policy and other applicable terms.

## 6. Third-Party Services

The Service may use the following third-party services:

- Apple App Store
- Google Play
- RevenueCat

Information handled by those services is subject to the privacy policies and other terms of the respective service providers.

## 7. AI Features

1. The Service may in the future provide AI-powered summarization, audio analysis, image or text recognition, handwriting recognition, conversation analysis, and similar functionality.
2. When such AI features are introduced, information selected by the User may be transmitted to an external AI service provider.
3. In such cases, the Operator will disclose the categories of data transmitted, the purpose of use, the recipient or service provider, and other material information through this Privacy Policy or feature-specific notices.
4. Information is not automatically transmitted to an external AI service for AI features that have not yet been launched.

## 8. Cloud Sync and Online Backup

1. The Service may in the future provide cloud synchronization or online backup functionality.
2. Before such features are launched, the Operator will disclose the categories of synchronized data, storage location, retention period, security measures, and other relevant information and will update this Privacy Policy where necessary.

## 9. Permissions for Photos, Camera, Microphone, Calendar, and Notifications

The Service may request access to the following device permissions where required to provide relevant features:

- Photo library
- Camera
- Microphone
- Calendar
- Notifications

These permissions are used only to the extent necessary to provide the applicable feature. Users can change permissions through the operating system settings.

## 10. Calendar Information

1. If a User uses calendar integration, the Service may add birthdays, anniversaries, outing schedules, or other information to the calendar, or may access calendar information to the extent necessary for the requested functionality.
2. Calendar access is performed only with the User’s permission.

## 11. Notifications

1. The Service may send reminders, service updates, or other notifications only after obtaining the User’s permission.
2. Depending on settings and usage, privacy-conscious notification display options may be provided.

## 12. Information About Third Parties

1. If a User records information about a third party in the Service, the User is responsible for handling that information lawfully and appropriately.
2. The Operator does not independently collect third-party information entered by Users.

## 13. Disclosure to Third Parties

The Operator will not disclose personal information under the Operator’s control to third parties except in the following cases:

1. With the individual’s consent
2. When disclosure is necessary to a service provider or contractor for operation of the Service and is limited to the necessary scope
3. When required by law
4. When necessary to protect a person’s life, body, or property and obtaining consent is difficult
5. When otherwise permitted by applicable law

## 14. Security Measures

1. The Operator takes reasonable security measures to prevent unauthorized access, leakage, loss, or damage to information handled by the Operator.
2. Authentication information such as startup passcodes is not stored in plaintext and is handled using secure storage mechanisms provided by the operating system or other appropriate methods.
3. However, the Operator does not guarantee absolute security of internet communications or electronic storage.

## 15. Retention

1. Information held by the Operator is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it is processed.
2. Information stored only on the User’s device may be deleted when the app is deleted, the device is reset, the User deletes the information, or through other device-level actions.
